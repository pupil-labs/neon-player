# Neon Player

Neon Player is a workspace for viewing and analyzing Neon recordings.

## Language

**Scene video**:
The recording's view of the environment in front of the wearer.
_Avoid_: Primary video, world video

**Eye video**:
The combined view of the eye camera images in a recording.

**Eye video panel**:
A view of the eye video that can overlay the scene video or occupy its own place in the viewing workspace.
_Avoid_: Eye overlay, when referring to the view regardless of its placement
